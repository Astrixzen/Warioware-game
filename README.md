 How to Play:-
 The game forces you through back-to-back mini challenges, with a brief 5-second breather screen in between to show you your remaining lives and level progress.Level 0 (Garlic Hunt): Move around the platformer stage and grab 3 hidden garlic items before your 10-second timer hits zero.Level 1 (Desktop Panic): The interface switches completely to a desktop UI grid. Use your mouse cursor to click and clear 4 annoying notification buttons inside a super tight 3-second time limit.
 CONTROLS-
 Jump-W
 Move left-A
 Move right-D
 Settings button is a W.I.P.
